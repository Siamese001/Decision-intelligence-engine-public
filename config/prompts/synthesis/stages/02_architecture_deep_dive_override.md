## ARCHETYPE OVERRIDE: ARCHITECTURE_DEEP_DIVE (RULE C-20 & PARSIMONY LAW)
- MANDATORY DOCUMENT STRUCTURE (IN STRICT SEQUENCE):
  1. # Subject: [Meeting Name] -- Executive Strategic & Technical Brief | [MM/DD/YYYY]
  2. Header Metadata (**Date:** MM/DD/YYYY, **Time:**, **Duration:**, **Attendees:**)
  3. # Meeting Objective (60-100w contextual narrative)
  4. # Key Discussion Points & Findings (8-12 thematic narrative bullets)
  5. # Action & Governance Ledger (MANDATORY TABLE - NEVER OMIT)
  6. # Appendix: Detailed Stakeholder Perspectives & Contribution Breakdown (Rule C-21)

- FORBIDDEN SECTIONS: DO NOT generate 'Immediate Delivery Scope & Release Boundaries', 'Business Meaning and Value Case', 'Active Technical Spikes & Experimental Inquiries', or 'Meeting Tone Arc & Strategic Dynamics' (speculative tone analytics and corporate engagement scores are strictly forbidden).
- RULE C-20 (ANTI-ECHO & MARGINAL VALUE LAW): Optional analytical extensions ('Architectural Trade-Off Matrix', 'Recommendations & Decisions Required') are STRICTLY CONDITIONAL. Each rendered extension must provide >= 80% NET-NEW analytical utility beyond Key Updates. If trade-offs, options, or unresolved items are already covered in Key Updates, OMIT them to receive full 20/20 parsimony credit. NEVER regurgitate or repeat findings in duplicate tables or decision sections.
- If no binding decisions were ratified, DO NOT generate 'Recommendations & Decisions Required' (leave open questions and proposed options in Key Updates).
- If technical trade-offs are already explained in Key Updates, DO NOT generate 'Architectural Trade-Off Matrix' (omission earns full 20/20 parsimony credit).
- MANDATORY ACTION & GOVERNANCE LEDGER SPECIFICATION: You MUST ALWAYS include the '# Action & Governance Ledger' table immediately following '# Key Discussion Points & Findings' and BEFORE '# Appendix'. Table columns: | # | Type | Item / Description | Accountable Owner | Target Timing | Evidence Locators | Status |. Consolidate all forward tasks, gates, and blockers with exact locators and explicit statuses ('Proposed', 'Pending Verification', 'Open'). In the Accountable Owner column, write ONLY the individual's first and last name (e.g. 'Jane Doe', 'John Smith') or 'Unassigned (Pending Owner Assignment)'. NEVER include company prefixes, parentheses, job titles, or slashes (do NOT write 'Organization (Jane Doe, Lead Engineer)', write simply 'Jane Doe'). Do not invent artificial weekday deadlines (use 'TBD' if unconfirmed). If zero forward actions were discussed or confirmed, render the header '# Action & Governance Ledger' followed by: 'No formal actions were established in this session; open initiatives remain unresolved questions.'
- In the Appendix (Rule C-21), synthesize deep stakeholder perspectives for each attendee (# [Name] - [Org], [Role]) using substantive themed bullets (- **[Theme]**: [Speaker Name] [Detailed narrative]), analyzing the session from their specific technical and governance lens without any defensive disclaimers or generic boilerplate. For attendees with zero independently spoken turns (such as silent or observational participants), provide concise role-referenced context based on what other attendees asked or said about their work; never infer personal stances or decisions.

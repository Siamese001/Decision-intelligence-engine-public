# Cognitive Blockers (14 Failure Modes)

A slide candidate is immediately **REJECTED** if any of the following 14 cognitive failure patterns are present:

1. **`primitive_but_valid`**: Technically adheres to geometry but looks like an amateur wireframe or raw vector dump.
2. **`box_dump`**: Multiple disconnected cards or containers without narrative or causal flow.
3. **`repetitive_peer_card_wall`**: Equal-sized peer cards used as a lazy substitute for structured analytical exhibits.
4. **`prose_first_layout`**: Paragraphs of text dominating the slide instead of visual diagrams and concise synthesized labels.
5. **`weak_dominant_exhibit`**: Main analytical exhibit fails to visibly anchor the slide or falls below 45% usable area.
6. **`unclear_reading_order`**: Eye is pulled in multiple directions simultaneously; lacks top-to-bottom or left-to-right hierarchy.
7. **`relationships_buried_in_prose`**: System connections, handoffs, or dependencies described in text rather than drawn visually.
8. **`semantic_icons_replaced_by_crude_glyphs`**: Inexpressive bullets, crude shapes, or decorative clipart that detracts from meaning.
9. **`multiple_competing_messages`**: Attempting to deliver multiple primary conclusions on a single slide.
10. **`excessive_horizontal_systems`**: Too many full-width parallel tracks that fragment vertical visual coherence.
11. **`duplicated_thesis`**: Restating the same conclusion in multiple banners, callouts, or subtitle blocks.
12. **`dead_space_imbalance`**: Asymmetric awkward voids caused by poor layout distribution.
13. **`ui_wireframe_stretched_to_billboard`**: Stretched low-density UI mocks placed onto a 4K executive canvas.
14. **`relationship_label_without_relationship`**: Floating arrows, lines, or badges that do not connect meaningful nodes.
